What Groundwork does

Everything in the product, grouped by the job it does: decide what to build, hand it to your tools, and learn whether it worked.

Decide what to buildBring evidence in, let it group itself, and see why one problem outranks another.

Evidence from anywhere
Paste notes, upload PDF or Word files, or import tickets from Zendesk. Uploaded files are size and page limited and read as untrusted input.
Automatic grouping
New evidence joins the closest problem, or forms a new one once enough has piled up. With an Anthropic key it uses Claude; without one it falls back to a plain keyword match. You can turn it off.
Transparent priorities
Each problem shows the pieces of its score: how much evidence, how many customers, how recent. No hidden weighting.
Out-of-date warnings
When new evidence arrives after a brief was written, the brief is marked as out of date so nobody builds from stale context.
Specs and plans
Draft a PRD, a launch plan or a clickable prototype from an initiative. Prototypes only ever render inside a sandboxed frame.
Weekly digest
A short list of findings about the workspace, such as stale briefs and what to build next. Uses one AI credit.

Hand it to your toolsWork leaves Groundwork as tickets and as context an agent can read.

Linear, Jira and Trello
Push an initiative as an issue or card. Status flows back, and only forward: a tracker never moves work backwards in Groundwork.
MCP server
11 tools let a coding agent read initiatives, problems and evidence, add evidence, update status and report outcomes. Each person has their own token and their role is enforced.
Webhooks and scheduled sync
Signed webhooks are checked for authenticity and replays. A scheduled job pulls status and imports new evidence.
Team and roles
Owner, admin, member and viewer. Invites are single-use, tied to one email address, and expire after 7 days.

Close the loopSay what you expected, then check what happened.

Outcomes
Record the result of shipped work against the problem it was meant to solve.
Agent activity
See what agents read and changed, including attempts that were denied.
Governance flags
Spot initiatives with no evidence behind them or no owner.
Your data stays yours
Export everything as JSON, delete your account, and read exactly how data is handled on the privacy page.

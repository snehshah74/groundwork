Stop guessing what to build. Trace every build back to what customers said.

Most teams keep feedback in one place, plans in another, and code in a third. By the time something ships, nobody can say which customer it was for or whether it worked.

Groundwork keeps the whole chain in one workspace. Each initiative links to its problem, its theme and its quotes, so your coding agent builds from the real situation, and you find out afterwards whether the metric moved.

Questions, answered

Do I need an Anthropic API key?

No. Without a key Groundwork still runs: it groups evidence with a simple text-similarity method and writes template specs. With a key, Claude does the grouping, specs, canvas, themes, stories and Ask Velo, and results are much sharper.

Which coding agents does it work with?

Any client that speaks MCP, including Claude Code and Cursor. If your tool does not, copy the brief from the Hand off tab and paste it in.

Is my data separate from other accounts?

Yes. Every account gets its own workspace database. Evidence is only sent to Claude when AI is switched on for the installation.

Can I get work into Jira, Linear or Trello?

Yes. Connect one under Integrations and push an initiative: it becomes an epic or parent issue with its stories beneath it, and status flows back. Asana and GitHub are next. For anything else, export a CSV of the epic and its stories.

How do I try it without my own data?

Sign up and choose "Load a demo workspace". It fills in a fictional invoicing product so you can walk the whole chain in a few minutes.

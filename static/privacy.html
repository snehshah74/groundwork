Data and privacy

A plain description of how the software handles data. This is not a legal policy.

What is stored

Your email, a hashed password if you use email sign-in, and the content you add: evidence, problems, initiatives, documents and connection settings. Each workspace lives in its own database schema.

Sign-in

Sign-in goes through Supabase Auth (Google, Microsoft or email). Groundwork verifies the result and keeps its own HttpOnly session cookie. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Integration credentials

Tokens for Linear, Jira, Trello and Zendesk are encrypted before they are saved. Customer details in imported tickets are scrubbed of obvious personal data.

AI

When AI is on, the text of the evidence or initiative being worked on is sent to Anthropic to produce the draft. When it is off, nothing is sent and basic offline drafts are used.

Third-party requests from this site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the sign-in pages load a script from jsDelivr. Those providers see your IP address.

Your controls

Owners can export all workspace data as JSON or delete the workspace. Anyone can delete their own account. Activity is recorded in an audit log that admins can read.

Questions or a deletion request you cannot do yourself: contact the person who runs this deployment.
